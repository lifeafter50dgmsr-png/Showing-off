# Velvet Dice

An intimate couples dice game with escalating heat levels.

## Warning
This game is for **consenting adults only**.  
It includes sensory play (temperature, touch, light impact, etc.).  
Anyone can pass or stop at any time — no explanation needed.

## Clean Play Options (Always Available)
- **Pass** — skip the card completely  
- **Soft Mode** — lower the intensity  
- **Switch** — change who gives and who receives  
- **Stop** — end the round or the game immediately  

These options override any dice roll or card.

## Heat Ladder
1. Mild  
2. Hot  
3. Spicy  
4. **Burned Your Tongue** (Green / Teal Keeper)  
5. **Tastes Like a Dill** (Purple Keeper)

## How to Play
1. Choose your starting heat level.
2. Roll the dice (or draw a card from the chosen deck).
3. Either player may use any Clean Play Option before or during the action.
4. After the action, the next player rolls or draws.
5. Consent stays at 100% the entire time.
